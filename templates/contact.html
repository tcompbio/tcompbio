Contact Information

Before contacting us, please make sure that your question is not answered in the FAQ. TCB is run by a small team of volunteers; please be mindful before addressing inquiries to the editorial team.

Please address any inquiries about the status of a paper under review to the Action Editor in charge of your paper. This can be done through OpenReview by submitting a comment and putting the AE as a reader. If your AE is not responsive, reach out to us at tcb-editors@tcompbio.org.

If you found issues on the TCB website, such as typos or broken links, please report them to the GitHub issue tracker.

For any other issues, please contact our editors-in-chief by emailing tcb-editors@tcompbio.org.

Organization

TCB is a self-published, volunteer-run open-access journal dedicated to computational biology.
